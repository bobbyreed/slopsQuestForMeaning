▶ play instructions — chapter 1

Estimated time: 30–45 minutes. Start a new game. Play through the following sequence, then come back and answer the questions below.

  • At the main menu terminal, type play to start.
  • Explore north to find The Prior at the shrine. Get your first ability.
  • Return to the overworld. Explore the dungeon to the south.
  • Visit The Prior's shop. Buy New Eyes to unlock the east and west worlds.
  • Clear the east region (find and beat The Render and The Pixel).
  • Clear the west region (find and beat The Duplicate).
  • Enter The Convergence to the north and complete it.
  • Beat The Prior in Joust. The world will change.

You can use the terminal command sudo to access developer shortcuts if you get stuck — but try to play it straight first.

▶ play instructions — chapter 2

Estimated time: 10–15 minutes. Continue directly from the end of the Joust. The world will change. Play through:

  • The opening gauntlet — side-scrolling, 8 enemies. Reach the far right edge.
  • The Clone encounter — boss fight. Reduce its HP to zero.
  • The Town — talk to the NPCs. Find the one who gives you a new ability.

Controls in Ch2: ← → or A D to move  ·  Z to attack  ·  Q for CORRUPT  ·  SPACE or ENTER to advance dialogue.

You cannot jump at the start of Ch2. That is intentional.
